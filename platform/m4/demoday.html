Now presenting

QUESTION 1
What did I build?
Say it like a headline. One or two sentences — what IS this thing?
QUESTION 2
Why did I build it?
The real reason. What made YOU pick this idea out of everything you could have made?
QUESTION 3
What did I learn?
One thing about building, one thing about AI, or one thing about yourself.
