About Code the Future

We built it for our own kids first. The mission is bigger than one age group.

A Louisville AI literacy initiative for older kids, young teens, and adults—so more people can understand artificial intelligence instead of feeling intimidated by it.

Learn by doing.

Real tools, practical judgment, developmentally thoughtful teaching, and humans who stay in control.

Explore programs

AI is being woven into school, work, communication, healthcare, creativity, and everyday decisions. At every age, the advantage begins with understanding it: how it works, what it is good at, where it can mislead you, and where it still needs a human.

Our mission

Code the Future teaches AI literacy and modern problem-solving through hands-on use, with humans retaining judgment and control. Our live founding program gives kids real AI experiences; the young-teen and adult tracks apply the same learn-by-doing philosophy at the right pace and level for each audience.

The skill that matters in an AI world is not memorizing answers. It is asking great questions, spotting when an answer is wrong, and knowing when to trust the machine. That is exactly what our game-like missions train.

How the current kids program works

A guided path from curiosity to something real.

01 · Meet

Small-group sessions

A weekly 90-minute session at the Louisville public library with a coach who works in the industry.

02 · Learn

Game-like missions

Twelve focused missions per module on our web platform, built around real AI tools.

03 · Build

Make and test

Kids turn their questions into projects, explain their choices, and improve what they made.

04 · Stay safe

Kid-safe by design

No child email required, filtered cohort chat, staff visibility, no ads, and a curriculum shaped with a child psychologist.
