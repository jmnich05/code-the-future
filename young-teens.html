Track forming · ages 12–15

Move from using AI to understanding how to direct it.

An upcoming track for young teens ready to research, create, code, and make better judgments with modern AI tools.

Young teens are already encountering AI in school, search, creative tools, and social platforms. This track is designed to replace passive use with informed, responsible building.

Planned learning arc

  • Research with AI without outsourcing judgment
  • Source checking, hallucination detection, and responsible attribution
  • Prompt and context design for complex projects
  • Creative building with text, images, data, and code
  • How agents and modern software workflows actually operate
  • Privacy, bias, digital identity, and appropriate boundaries

What we are forming

Format

Project-led workshops

Small cohorts working through real challenges, collaborative builds, and individual capstone projects.
