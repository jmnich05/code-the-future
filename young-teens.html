Track forming · Ages 12–15

Move from using AI to understanding how to direct it.

An upcoming track for young teens ready to research, create, code, and make better judgments with modern AI tools.

Interest list first

We are gathering Louisville families and teens before locking dates, session cadence, and the first cohort.

Planned learning arc

Replace passive use with informed building.

Young teens already encounter AI in school, search, creative tools, and social platforms. This track is designed to help them use it deliberately and responsibly.

Research with judgment

Use AI to explore a question without outsourcing the thinking or accepting an answer at face value.

Check the source

Practice source checking, hallucination detection, and responsible attribution.

Design the context

Give clear prompts, useful background, constraints, and feedback for more complex projects.

Create across formats

Build with text, images, data, and code while keeping the learner’s own intent at the center.

Understand workflows

See how agents and modern software workflows operate, including where people still need to direct and check the work.

Know the boundaries

Examine privacy, bias, digital identity, responsible use, and what should not be handed to an AI tool.

Project-led workshops

A repeatable way to think and build.

The forming track centers on small cohorts, real challenges, collaborative builds, and individual capstone projects.

STEP 01

Research

Frame the question, gather context, and use AI to widen the search without handing over the final judgment.

STEP 02

Verify

Check sources, compare claims, test assumptions, and identify where the output may be incomplete or wrong.

STEP 03

Build

Turn the idea into a project with text, images, data, or code, then improve it through iteration.

STEP 04

Explain

Present what was made, how AI contributed, which decisions stayed human, and what should change next.

What we are forming
