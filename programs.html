One mission · three starting points

AI makes more sense when you can use it, question it, and build with it.

Code the Future creates hands-on AI learning for older kids, young teens, and adults. Each program meets learners at the right pace while keeping practical judgment and human control at the center.

Built in Louisville

The ages 8–11 program is live. Young-teen and adult tracks are forming from local interest.

Hands-on first

Learners work through real examples and make things instead of only hearing explanations about AI.

Judgment over memorization

They practice asking better questions, checking answers, protecting private information, and knowing when to verify.

Plain language

Concepts come before vocabulary. Real terms are explained clearly, without talking down to the learner.
