A hands-on Louisville program where older kids use real AI, practice judgment, and create things they are proud to explain.

The current Code the Future program combines game-like online missions with small-group sessions led by an industry operator and shaped with a child psychologist.

What learners practice

  • Writing clear instructions and improving weak prompts
  • Spotting wrong or misleading answers
  • Digital privacy, fairness, attribution, and responsible use
  • Building creative projects with real AI tools
