The stages (14 screens · ~30 min)

TimeStageHow to run it
3 min1 · LobbyKids log in and open the live page — names pop onto the projector as they land. Hype each arrival by name.
3 min2 · Warm-up vote
"AI is most like…"		Opinion poll, no right answer. Ask a defender of each option to say why. Reveal shows the discussion note (patterns + puppy-training are the camp answers).
6 min3 · Movie time 🍿
Code.org "How AI Works"		Group watch on the projector (~5 min) — kid screens show "eyes up, devices down." Press play yourself; Next when it ends.
8 min4–9 · AI or NOT?
6 rounds · game show		The scored game — YouTube recs, calculator, face unlock, flashlight, spam filter, dice. Keep it fast and loud. Points auto-tally for the leaderboard.
5 min10–12 · Mythbusters
3 rounds		"AI knows everything" · "AI can make mistakes" · "AI has feelings" — True/False/Sort-of. The reveals carry the AI-literacy punchlines; let each one breathe for 30 seconds of talk.
6 min13 · Pick your Big Build 🌉The finale — now fully IN the room: their devices show the 12-build menu right on the live page (pick → personalize → lock in), and every pick pops onto the projector with their hero — "Ava · Sparky the baby dragon!" Cheer every pick. Kids can change their pick any time before build week.
1 min14 · Leaderboard + wrapTop scores get medals on screen; every kid sees their own score on their device. Point them to the week's missions on the platform.

Before the session

  • Staff login done on the projector laptop (Staff tab) → open the host URL, confirm "0 in the room" shows.
  • Kid tablets charged + logged in with their codes (a kid who isn't logged in will hit the sign-in page first — that's fine, it forwards back).
  • Library wifi test: open the kid URL on one tablet, confirm the name pops on the host lobby.
  • Projector: use the browser's full-screen mode (F11 / ⌃⌘F).
  • Optional: print the Big Build menu board (big-build-menu.html) or keep it in a second tab to walk the 12 options before the pick.
