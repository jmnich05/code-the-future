Module 1 · Class 1 — "What Is AI?"

A 90-minute in-person session at the library. First half is talking — open questions, kids in their own words. Second half is building — tablets on the platform. A 10-minute break splits them.

🟣 Talk it out0:00 – 0:40 · screens down
🚻 Break0:40 – 0:50
🟢 Build it0:50 – 1:30 · screens up
The one big idea (everything points here)

AI isn't magic — it's a machine that learns from examples, spots patterns, and predicts. It's powerful, it makes mistakes, and you stay the boss of it.

Part 1

Talk it out — screens down

40 min
0:00–0:08

Welcome to the Guild

Get every voice in the room before anything else.
  • Gather in a circle (chairs or floor). Welcome them as "AI Apprentices."
  • Name + spark: each kid says their name and one thing they think AI can do.
  • Set the rule of the day: "There are no wrong answers — we're explorers."
0:08–0:24

Open floor: "What even is AI?" the heart of today

Pure discussion. Toss out open questions, follow their energy, get them talking in their own words. Use the question bank below — pick whatever the room runs with.
  • Catch a great line and write it on the whiteboard — you'll come back to it.
  • Keep it moving with the facilitator moves in the sidebar ("Say more…", "Who sees it differently?").
🗣️ Open-question bank — for the first half

Ask these like a curious friend, not a quiz. Let them argue a little.

  1. Where have you seen or used AI? (phones, games, YouTube, Alexa…)
  2. If you had to explain AI to a 5-year-old, what would you say?
  3. Is a video-game bad guy "AI"? Why or why not?
  4. Can AI think like you do? What's the same — what's different?
  5. Where does AI get its smarts — is it born knowing, or does it learn?
  6. Can AI ever be wrong? Have you ever caught it making a mistake?
  7. How do you think AI learns — who teaches it?
  8. Should there be things AI is NOT allowed to do?
  9. Is AI alive? Does it have feelings?
  10. What's something you wish AI could make or do for you?
0:24–0:34

Unplugged: "The Secret Rule"

A game that teaches "AI learns from examples" — with zero screens.
  • You are the AI. You have a secret rule (e.g., you "like" round things, or words with the letter S).
  • Kids call out examples; you answer yes / no; they shout the rule when they crack it.
  • Flip it: a kid invents a rule and "trains" the room.
  • Land it in their words: "That's how AI learns — from examples, not magic. If I only saw bad examples, I'd learn the wrong thing."
0:34–0:40

"Bad AI / Good AI"

Quick, funny, all talking — sets up next week's prompting.
  • "If you told a robot 'make me a game,' what might go wrong?"
  • "How could you ask better?" (more detail = better result)
  • Land it: clear words get better results — and you're the boss of the AI.
🚻
0:40–0:50 · 10 minutes

Break — bathroom, water, wiggles

Hard stop. Come back ready to power up the tablets.
Part 2

Build it — screens up

40 min
0:50–0:56

Power up

  • Tablets out. Log in with their code; finish their character if they haven't.
  • Quick "everybody in?" check before you launch missions together.
0:56–1:18

Play the Module 1 missions on their own screens

The same ideas from the first half — now hands-on. You circulate, celebrate, help.
  • AI or Not? — The Arcade — spot AI in real life
  • Say hi to a real AI — their first chat
  • Be a Pattern Detective + Train your own AI — the Secret Rule, now on screen
  • 🎨 Draw something — a REAL AI will guess it! — the showpiece
1:18–1:25

Group reveal — on your projection

  • 2–3 volunteers project their Draw mission; the whole room watches the AI guess live.
  • Cheer the hits, laugh at the misses → "See? It's smart AND it makes mistakes."
1:25–1:30

Land the plane

  • Back to a circle, screens down. Each kid: "I used to think ___, now I know ___."
  • "Your missions keep going at home this week — finish Module 1 on the platform."
  • Hand out the Apprentice badge / stamp. See you next week!

🎯 The 6 Module 1 ideas

Name these out loud whenever a kid stumbles into one.
  • 1 AI is everywhere around us
  • 2 It learns from examples
  • 3 It finds patterns
  • 4 It predicts — words & pictures
  • 5 It makes mistakes
  • 6 You're the boss of it

💬 Discussion moves

  • • "Say more about that."
  • • "Who agrees? Who sees it differently?"
  • • "Explain it like I'm 5."
  • • Echo their words back to the room.
  • • "Turn to your neighbor" for 30 seconds, then share.
  • • Energy dipping? Stand up, switch to the Secret Rule game.

🎒 What to bring

  • ○ Circle of chairs / a rug
  • ○ Whiteboard + markers
  • ○ Tablets — charged & logged in (codes ready)
  • ○ Projector + the platform open in a tab
  • ○ Apprentice badges / stamps
  • ○ Sticky notes (optional)

⏱️ The shape

  • 40 min talk (screens down)
  • 10 min break
  • 40 min build (screens up)
  • If discussion runs hot, let it — you can trim a mission, not the talking.
