🛠️ Module 4 · Your Big Build

What will YOU build this summer?

In the last week of camp you'll build a real app or game with a real AI — layer by layer, checkpoint by checkpoint across the Big Four Bridge — and it'll be yours to own: present it to your family and keep it forever. Pick your dream, then make it yours.

🎮 Games
🎮

Tap-It Clicker

Tap to score points, buy upgrades, chase the high score.
✨ Make it yours
What do you tap — a cookie? a dragon egg? a soccer ball? What's the theme?
🧺

Catch It!

Slide left and right — catch the good stuff, dodge the bad.
✨ Make it yours
What falls from the sky — tacos? stars? puppies? Who catches them?
🚀

Dodge & Fly

Fly through obstacles. Don't crash. How long can you last?
✨ Make it yours
What are you flying — a rocket? a dragon? Where — space? a volcano?
🏃

Endless Runner

Run, jump the obstacles, collect coins, beat your best.
✨ Make it yours
Who's running — a ninja? your cat? Through a jungle? your school? Mars?
🧩

Memory Match

Flip the cards, find the pairs, race the clock.
✨ Make it yours
Cards about anything you love — animals? planets? your own family?
❓

Quiz Show

Your own game show — you write the questions!
✨ Make it yours
A quiz about what you're the expert in — dinosaurs? soccer? your life?

Art Studio

Beat Maker

Tap colorful pads to make music and record your beats.
✨ Make it yours
What sounds — drums? space bloops? animal noises? Pick your DJ name!
🏰 Stories & Worlds
📖

Choose-Your-Adventure

A story where the reader decides what happens — multiple endings!
✨ Make it yours
Who's the hero — YOU? a lost robot? Where does it start — a spooky castle? the moon?

World Builder

Build a whole place people can explore by clicking around.

🌟 Example dream

"A virtual pet app where I take care of Sparky the baby dragon who lives in a candy volcano — and he sneezes glitter."

Pick one today — you can change your mind anytime before build week.
Every project gets: your own hero & world · a score or goal · sounds & effects · your style · a start screen · a finished app that's all yours.
