10:32–11:02🏢 THE BIG GAME — AI Business Tycoon

The why (your study notes)
This is Module 2 turned into a game. The kids get $1,000 and start ONE company together — every decision is a group vote on their devices, and every option secretly maps to a module lesson: confidently-wrong AI (humans handle exceptions), selling user data (privacy), pricing (fairness/recess balls), AI-does-everything (generic in, generic out). Money is the flashy score, but Trust ❤️ is the real one — kind choices literally pay off later in the game (the storm round pays trust back).
Say it like this
"Every company you love started exactly like this: a little money, a big idea, and a thousand decisions. Today YOU are the board of directors. Majority vote rules. Spend wisely — and watch the hearts, because trust is a kind of money too."
How to run it (host = projector, staff login)
  • Open codethefuture.net/platform/live/tycoon.html on the projector; kids open the same URL on devices (their view auto-becomes voting tiles).
  • Controls: Next › advances · tap a row to hand-pick (tie-breaks/overrides) · Lock it in 🔒 applies the winning vote, moves the money, shows the outcome. Keys: space/→/←, L = lock.
  • Flow: intro → vote the company (10 choices, all AI-for-good) → name it (they shout, you type — this moment is pure gold) → 6 board-meeting votes + 1 storm disaster → demo-day finale with valuation, hearts, and every lesson they collected.
  • Before each Lock In: take 60 seconds of debate. "Someone argue for the risky one. Someone argue against." THEN vote. The arguing is the learning.
  • After each outcome: connect it back — "that's the hammer thing / that's the diary thing / that's humans-handle-exceptions."
  • If money tanks: an emergency investor auto-rescues them once at $150 — play it dramatic ("one more chance, board…"). Losing rounds are GREAT — real founders lose money learning.
  • Budget ~3–4 min per decision → ~30 min total. Running long? Skip the office round (Final move works from anywhere — just hit Next past one).

Appendix📡 Trivia answer key — the WHY behind all 20

Order matches the deck. Each has the answer, the deeper reason, and a follow-up to ask before or after Reveal. Mission numbers = where they learned it this week.

🌍 Warm-up · "Point AI at ONE big problem…"opinion

No wrong answer — a thermometer. Note the room's favorite; when the Tycoon business menu appears at 10:32, call it back: "half of you voted planet — PlantPal and NoWaste are right there."

Q1 · AI is like which old invention?💡 Electricity

Why (M2): electricity began as a novelty bulb people stared at, then slipped quietly behind everything — lights, fridges, hospitals. AI is mid-slip right now: from "wow, a chatbot" to running behind hospitals, farms, and cities. That's why this module exists: things THAT common have to be used well.

Ask: "When did you last think about electricity?" (Never — it disappeared into the walls. AI is disappearing into the world the same way, in their lifetime.)

Q2 · Farm drone that learns thirsty plants🤖 AI

Why (M2): it LEARNS from examples — thirsty vs. happy plants — and improves. Last week's master question still rules everything: does it get better with practice?

Ask: "What are its training examples?" (Millions of plant photos + which ones turned out to need water.)

Q3 · Paper map of Louisville🚫 NOT AI

Why (M2): same ink forever — never learns, never updates. Useful ≠ smart. Nice contrast pair with Q2 on purpose.

Ask: "What would the map need to become AI-ish?" (Learn from traffic, reroute you, get better — congrats, you invented the maps app.)

Q4 · AI helping a doctor is like a…🔍 Metal detector

Why (M3): a metal detector doesn't dig or decide — it BEEPS so the human knows where to look. AI that studied millions of scans points at faint patterns a tired human eye might miss. Earlier catches = earlier help.

Misconception to catch: "robot doctor." No — helper, never boss.

Ask: "What does the beep NOT tell you?" (What it means or what to do — that's the doctor's job.)

Q5 · Who makes the FINAL call on a patient?🧑‍⚕️ Human doctor

Why (M3): caring for a person takes judgment and kindness — human superpowers. This is the module's spine and it comes back in Tycoon's "add a human check" round.

Ask: "Why not let the AI decide if it studied MORE scans than any doctor?" (Because knowing patterns isn't the same as caring for THIS person — the exception might be sitting in the chair.)

Q6 · How does AI help invent medicine?🔑 Tests millions of ideas fast

Why (M3): like trying a million keys in a lock in the time it used to take to try a few. Scientists still choose the lock and check the key that turns.

Ask: "Somebody in this room could be the scientist who cracks a disease with an AI helper. What disease would you point it at?"

Q7 · Why is AI good at storm warnings?📚 Studied a billion storms

Why (M4): nobody sees tomorrow — forecasting is patterns from a zillion past days. Same trick as everything else: examples in, predictions out. Earlier warnings save lives.

Ask: "Is the AI ever WRONG about a storm?" (Yes — prediction, not prophecy. That's why humans still make the call to evacuate.)

Q8 · AI cracked a 50-year science puzzle✅ TRUE

Why (M4): protein shapes — the tiny machines inside living things. Scientists were stuck for ~50 years; AI + scientists cracked nearly all of them. Helps medicine, food, understanding nature.

Ask: "Did the AI do it alone?" (No — scientists built it, aimed it, checked it. Team win.)

Q9 · "Code handles the ___"🔁 Loop

Why (M5): the camp's whole thesis begins here. Code repeats perfectly and never gets bored — ten thousand emails, no yawns.

Ask: tee up Q10 — "so who's on deck for the weird stuff…?"

Q10 · Who handles the EXCEPTIONS?🧑 Humans

Why (M5): the strange, sad, brand-new stuff the AI has never seen needs care and judgment. Full rule on the board: code does the loop, AI does the judgment inside the loop, humans handle the exceptions. They'll live this rule inside Tycoon in 20 minutes.

Ask: "Which part sounds like the job YOU'D want?"

📊 HALFTIME BOARDno points

New this week. Names + scores, live. Hype line: "ten questions left — the trophy has changed hands at halftime before…" Then breathe; you're halfway on the clock too.

Q11 · Sad tricky email shows up🧑 A human steps in

Why (M5): that's an EXCEPTION. The AI took the boring 9,999 precisely so a person has time for the one that matters. Kindness doesn't automate.

Ask: "Why not let the AI answer it — it's read a billion emails?" (Reading about sad ≠ caring. The person on the other end deserves a person.)

Q12 · "The dishwasher ended cooking"MYTH

Why (M5): the dishwasher freed the cook to COOK more. AI is that for lots of jobs — it eats the repeating part; the caring/deciding/creating part grows. And brand-new jobs appear: teaching AIs, checking AIs, deciding their rules. "App maker" wasn't a job when your parents were kids.

Ask: "What job might exist when YOU'RE 25 that doesn't exist today?" (Board the wild ones — some will be right.)

Q13 · Is AI good or bad?🔨 Neither — choices matter

Why (M6): the hammer test. Treehouse or broken window — same hammer. AI is the same, just vastly more powerful, so the choices matter vastly more.

Ask: "Give me one thing in your house that passes the hammer test." (Everything does. That's the point.)

Q14 · "Should we?" and…⚖️ "Who gets to choose?"

Why (M6): the two grown-up questions. CAN ≠ SHOULD, and who decides shapes who benefits. These two questions run the entire back half of Tycoon.

Ask: "Name something we CAN do but maybe shouldn't." (Their examples get deep fast — let one breathe.)

Q15 · Your trail online = digital ___👣 Footprint

Why (M7): watch, search, type, go — little trails everywhere. Not scary, just real: AI learns from data like yours, so you should be the boss of what you leave behind.

Ask: "What did YOU add to your footprint this morning before 9am?" (YouTube, a game login, a search…)

Q16 · Your home address is a…📔 Diary thing

Why (M7): bulletin board = favorite color, proud drawings; diary = address, passwords, where you'll be Saturday. Thoughtful, not scared.

Ask: lightning-sort a few out loud — "favorite team?" (board) "your school's name plus your full name?" (careful — that's the mixer question sneaking up later 😏)

Q17 · One kid owns every ball at recess⚖️ Not fair — even if nice

Why (M8): no villain needed — even a nice kid owning everything means everyone else has to come asking. The biggest AI tools today are held by a few companies/countries because they're insanely expensive. Fairness question, not a monster question: tools that shape the world should help everybody.

Ask: "How does it get fairer?" (More people learning it, using it, having a say — "which is literally you, on a Saturday, at a library.")

Q18 · What keeps powerful tools HELPING?📜 Good rules + many voices

Why (M9): the flashlight — lights your path home, or gets shined in your face. Tools don't decide; PEOPLE do. Good rules (like traffic lights — rules can be kind!) and many voices keep the light on the path.

Handle with care: if a kid brings up scary surveillance stuff, validate ("big thought — allowed") and land on the hopeful mechanics: rules, voices, people like them.

Ask: "What's a rule that makes recess MORE fun, not less?" (Taking turns — rules can be the kind thing.)

Q19 · Who decides the future of AI?🌍 Nobody alone — incl. YOU

Why (M10): not one company, not one ruler. Rules made together, voting, speaking up, new builders with fresh ideas. The future of AI is NOT decided yet — it's being figured out while they're kids, which means their voice is in it.

Ask: "If you could make ONE rule every AI must follow, what is it?" (Gold — write the best on the whiteboard and leave it up all session.)

Q20 · Great builder = curiosity + kindness + ___🧠 Judgment

Why (M11): the module's summit. AI brings speed; the human brings wisdom — should we, is it fair, who could this hurt. A robot can sort a million photos; only a person decides which idea DESERVES building.

Ask (right before the champion board): "Which of the three do you already have the most of?" Then reveal, crown the champ, hand out the candy and stickers. 🏆

🕵️ Mixer key · Help or Hurt? (after the video)

1 · Book AI locked to ONE kind forever → 😬 Could hurt. Recommendations are great; a forever-bubble means never discovering anything. Same tool, different setup — the choices matter (M6). Ask: "How would you set it up instead?" (Mostly what I love + a surprise sometimes.)

2 · City energy saver → 🙌 Helping. Huge-number problems are AI's home turf (M4); humans picked the goal, AI executes it better.

3 · Watches everyone all the time → 😬 Could hurt. The flashlight in your face (M9). Not a fear beat — a WHY-rules-matter beat.

🔒 Mixer key · Share or Keep?

1 · Favorite color → 📌 Share. Being the boss of your info ≠ hiding everything.

2 · Password → 🔒 Keep. Always. No app, no game, no "friend online" ever needs it. Ever.

3 · Project photo + full name + school → 🧑 Ask first. The best reflex in the whole module: when it's a MIX, ask a grown-up you trust. Not babyish — exactly what smart people do.
