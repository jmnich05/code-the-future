Appendix📡 Trivia answer key — the WHY behind all 10

Order matches the deck. Each has the answer, the deeper reason, and a follow-up to ask before or after Reveal. Mission numbers = where they learned it this week.

🌍 Warm-up · "Point AI at ONE big problem…"opinion

No wrong answer — a thermometer. Note the room's favorite; when the Tycoon business menu appears at 10:32, call it back: "half of you voted planet — PlantPal and NoWaste are right there."

Q1 · AI is like which old invention?💡 Electricity

Why (M2): electricity began as a novelty bulb people stared at, then slipped quietly behind everything — lights, fridges, hospitals. AI is mid-slip right now: from "wow, a chatbot" to running behind hospitals, farms, and cities. That's why this module exists: things THAT common have to be used well.

Ask: "When did you last think about electricity?" (Never — it disappeared into the walls. AI is disappearing into the world the same way, in their lifetime.)

Q2 · Farm drone that learns thirsty plants🤖 AI

Why (M2): it LEARNS from examples — thirsty vs. happy plants — and improves. Last week's master question still rules everything: does it get better with practice?

Ask: "What are its training examples?" (Millions of plant photos + which ones turned out to need water.)

Q3 · AI helping a doctor is like a…🔍 Metal detector

Why (M3): a metal detector doesn't dig or decide — it BEEPS so the human knows where to look. AI that studied millions of scans points at faint patterns a tired human eye might miss. Earlier catches = earlier help.

Misconception to catch: "robot doctor." No — helper, never boss.

Ask: "What does the beep NOT tell you?" (What it means or what to do — that's the doctor's job.)

Q4 · Why is AI good at storm warnings?📚 Studied a billion storms

Why (M4): nobody sees tomorrow — forecasting is patterns from a zillion past days. Same trick as everything else: examples in, predictions out. Earlier warnings save lives.

Ask: "Is the AI ever WRONG about a storm?" (Yes — prediction, not prophecy. That's why humans still make the call to evacuate.)

Q5 · "Code handles the ___"🔁 Loop

Why (M5): the camp's whole thesis begins here. Code repeats perfectly and never gets bored — ten thousand emails, no yawns.

Ask: tee up Q6, right after the halftime board — "so who's on deck for the weird stuff…?"

📊 HALFTIME BOARDno points

New this week. Names + scores, live. Hype line: "five questions left — the trophy has changed hands at halftime before…" Then breathe; you're halfway on the clock too.

Q6 · Who handles the EXCEPTIONS?🧑 Humans

Why (M5): the strange, sad, brand-new stuff the AI has never seen needs care and judgment. Full rule on the board: code does the loop, AI does the judgment inside the loop, humans handle the exceptions. They'll live this rule inside Tycoon in 20 minutes.

Ask: "Which part sounds like the job YOU'D want?"

Q7 · Is AI good or bad?🔨 Neither — choices matter

Why (M6): the hammer test. Treehouse or broken window — same hammer. AI is the same, just vastly more powerful, so the choices matter vastly more.

Ask: "Give me one thing in your house that passes the hammer test." (Everything does. That's the point.)

Q8 · Your trail online = digital ___👣 Footprint

Why (M7): watch, search, type, go — little trails everywhere. Not scary, just real: AI learns from data like yours, so you should be the boss of what you leave behind.

Ask: "What did YOU add to your footprint this morning before 9am?" (YouTube, a game login, a search…)

Q9 · One kid owns every ball at recess⚖️ Not fair — even if nice

Why (M8): no villain needed — even a nice kid owning everything means everyone else has to come asking. The biggest AI tools today are held by a few companies/countries because they're insanely expensive. Fairness question, not a monster question: tools that shape the world should help everybody.

Ask: "How does it get fairer?" (More people learning it, using it, having a say — "which is literally you, on a Saturday, at a library.")

Q10 · Great builder = curiosity + kindness + ___🧠 Judgment

Why (M11): the module's summit. AI brings speed; the human brings wisdom — should we, is it fair, who could this hurt. A robot can sort a million photos; only a person decides which idea DESERVES building.

Ask (right before the champion board): "Which of the three do you already have the most of?" Then reveal, crown the champ, hand out the candy and stickers. 🏆

🕵️ Mixer key · Help or Hurt? (after the video)

1 · Book AI locked to ONE kind forever → 😬 Could hurt. Recommendations are great; a forever-bubble means never discovering anything. Same tool, different setup — the choices matter (M6). Ask: "How would you set it up instead?" (Mostly what I love + a surprise sometimes.)

2 · City energy saver → 🙌 Helping. Huge-number problems are AI's home turf (M4); humans picked the goal, AI executes it better.

3 · Watches everyone all the time → 😬 Could hurt. The flashlight in your face (M9). Not a fear beat — a WHY-rules-matter beat.

🔒 Mixer key · Share or Keep?

1 · Favorite color → 📌 Share. Being the boss of your info ≠ hiding everything.

2 · Password → 🔒 Keep. Always. No app, no game, no "friend online" ever needs it. Ever.

3 · Project photo + full name + school → 🧑 Ask first. The best reflex in the whole module: when it's a MIX, ask a grown-up you trust. Not babyish — exactly what smart people do.
