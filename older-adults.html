Track forming · Older adults

Understand AI without being rushed, patronized, or overwhelmed.

Patient, practical Louisville workshops for using modern AI safely and confidently in everyday life.

A practical introduction

This is not a technical boot camp. It starts with what AI is useful for, where it can mislead you, and how to stay in control.

Join the interest list

Planned workshop topics

Use what helps. Understand what needs checking.

The workshops are designed around useful, repeatable practice—not jargon or pressure to become a technical expert.

What assistants do

Understand what ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI assistants actually do.

Ask useful questions

Give better context, make a clear request, and improve an answer through follow-up.

Recognize the risks

Check answers, notice scams, and protect private information before sharing anything with a tool.

Make daily tasks easier

Try AI for letters, planning, travel, learning, hobbies, and organization.

Understand synthetic media

Learn how AI images, voice cloning, misinformation, and deepfakes can appear in everyday life.

Build safe routines

Create a personal set of useful, repeatable tasks while keeping clear boundaries around what AI should not do.

Hands-on practice

Try it slowly. Stay in control.

Bring your own device, work through real examples, repeat steps, and ask every question without jargon.

STEP 01

Try

Start with a useful everyday task and see what the assistant produces.

STEP 02

Check

Review the answer, look for mistakes, and know when to confirm information somewhere else.

STEP 03

Protect

Practice what never to share, how to recognize manipulation, and when a human should stay in charge.

STEP 04

Repeat

Save the safe, useful patterns that work so the next task feels familiar instead of overwhelming.

How we want it to feel

Patient enough to practice. Clear enough to use again.

The goal is confidence with boundaries: knowing what to try, what to verify, what never to share, and when a human should remain in charge.
