Track forming · older adults

Understand AI without being rushed, patronized, or overwhelmed.

Patient, practical Louisville workshops for using modern AI safely and confidently in everyday life.

This is not a technical boot camp. It is a guided introduction to what AI is, what it is useful for, where it can mislead you, and how to stay in control while using it.

Planned workshop topics

  • What ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and AI assistants actually do
  • Writing useful questions and giving better context
  • Checking answers, recognizing scams, and protecting private information
  • Using AI for letters, planning, travel, learning, hobbies, and organization
  • Understanding AI images, voice cloning, misinformation, and deepfakes
  • Building a personal set of safe, repeatable uses

How we want it to feel

Pace

Patient and hands-on

Bring your own device, work through real examples, repeat steps, and ask every question without jargon.

Outcome

Confidence with boundaries

Leave knowing what to try, what to verify, what never to share, and when a human should stay in charge.
